AI Expense Tracker

A phone app for keeping track of expenses for bookkeeping. Snap a photo of a receipt, upload a PDF, or just say what you spent, and AI fills in the vendor, amount, date, category and payment method. It works offline and keeps everything ready for your accountant.

Open the app

What it does

  • Reads receipt photos and spoken or typed descriptions of purchases, using AI services the user connects with their own accounts.
  • Stores expenses on the user's phone, and works without a connection.
  • Exports a spreadsheet (Excel or CSV) for the user's records or accountant.
  • Optionally adds each expense to a Google Sheet the user creates or chooses, with receipt photos saved to their Google Drive.

How it uses Google

If you choose to connect Google Sheets, the app asks only for access to the files it creates or that you pick (Google's "drive.file" permission). It uses that access only to add, update and remove your expense rows and to save your receipt photos to a folder in your Drive. It can't see any of your other files.
